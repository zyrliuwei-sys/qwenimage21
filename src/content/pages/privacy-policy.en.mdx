## About this policy

This policy describes the QwenImage AI site at qwenimage-21.org, including the studio where signed-in users generate and edit images with credits.

## Prompts, reference images and generated images

When you generate or edit an image, your prompt, chosen options and any reference images you upload are sent to our image provider, fal.ai, which runs the Qwen Image model. fal.ai may process them under its own terms. The images that come back are stored with your account so you can find them again under "Your recent images" and download them. Your unsent draft is kept only in your browser tab.

## Accounts and support

If you create an account, we process the details needed for authentication and account management, such as your email address and session. If you send a support request, we process the information you provide to answer it. The server may receive standard request information, including IP address and browser details, for operation and security.

Generation requires an account. Payments are handled by our payment provider (such as Stripe, Creem or PayPal); we receive the order and subscription status but never your full card details. Analytics and support integrations may also process information needed for the features you use.

## Retention and your choices

Generated images, prompts, credit and order records are kept while your account is active and as needed to meet accounting and legal requirements. You can ask us to delete your account and its images through the support area after signing in.

## Updates

We may revise this policy as the site changes. The date above shows the latest revision.
